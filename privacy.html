Privacy Policy

Max Level Earth Studio · Effective 11 November 2026

What this app is

Max Level Earth Studio is a private desktop tool used only by the owner of the Max Level Earth channel. It uploads and schedules the channel's own videos on YouTube and reads the channel's own statistics. It is not offered to the public and does not access any other person's account.

Data the app accesses

After the channel owner signs in with Google and grants permission, the app uses the YouTube API Services to:

  • upload videos and set their titles, descriptions, tags and publish times (youtube.upload);
  • read the channel's videos and their public statistics (youtube.readonly);
  • read YouTube Analytics reports for the channel, such as views and watch time (yt-analytics.readonly).

How the data is used and stored

  • The data is used only to publish the channel's videos at their scheduled times and to show their performance in a dashboard that runs on the owner's own computer.
  • The OAuth token and the statistics are stored only on the owner's computer. They are not sold, shared, transferred or used for advertising.
  • Data is kept only while the authorization is active; it is deleted if access is revoked, in line with the YouTube API Services Developer Policies.

Google and YouTube

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact options on the Max Level Earth YouTube channel.
